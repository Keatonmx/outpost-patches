Tinbox: Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

Tinbox is a GBA, GB and GBC emulator for iPhone by Redfern's Outpost. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information. This page explains exactly what the app keeps, where, and the two optional features that use the network.

What Tinbox collects

Nothing. Tinbox has no accounts of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no telemetry.

What stays on your phone

  • Your games, saves, states, cheats, screenshots and settings are stored in the app's own folder, visible in the Files app under On My iPhone, then Tinbox, or in a folder you choose. Deleting the app deletes the files in its own folder; the Back up feature in Settings lets you keep a copy elsewhere.
  • Motion sensor data, for games that use tilt or gyro cartridges, is used live and never stored.
  • Speedrun times and Time Capsule snapshots are saved alongside the game they belong to.

Optional network features

Tinbox only uses the network when you turn on or use one of these:

Box art

Settings › Library › Box art, on by default. For games that have no cover, Tinbox requests an image from the open-source libretro-thumbnails project hosted on GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). The request contains only the game's file name. You can turn this off at any time; it is never used for anything else.

RetroAchievements

Optional sign-in. If you sign in, your username and password are sent directly and only to retroachievements.org to obtain a session token, which is stored on your phone so you stay signed in. While you are signed in, Tinbox sends gameplay progress to retroachievements.org so achievements can unlock on your account: a hash (fingerprint) of each game you open, which achievements you earn and when, and a short "now playing" status every couple of minutes while a game is running. No game files, saves or screenshots are sent, and nothing is sent while you are signed out. Signing out deletes the token and stops all of this. RetroAchievements' own privacy policy applies to their service.

No other servers are contacted. Tinbox never uploads your games or saves.

Camera

If you play a Game Boy Camera cartridge, Tinbox asks for camera permission the first time the cartridge requests a frame. Camera frames are handed directly to the emulated cartridge on your phone, exactly like pointing the original hardware at something. Nothing is stored by Tinbox and nothing leaves your phone. No other feature uses the camera.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts of its own, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No third party code that collects data. The only outside components are the open-source mGBA and rcheevos libraries, which run entirely on your phone.
  • No access to your contacts, photos (beyond a cover you pick yourself), location or microphone.

Children

Tinbox does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.
