Tinbox

A pocket tin full of games. GBA, GB and GBC on your iPhone, with no ads, no accounts and no paid version.

Tinbox plays legally obtained game files and comes with two free homebrew games so there is something to play the moment the lid opens. Everything you add stays in plain folders on your phone.

Get it on the App Store.

Questions

How do I add my own games?

Tap the Import ROM tile in the Library and pick a .gba, .gb, .gbc or zipped game file from the Files app. Tinbox plays legally obtained game files; it does not download games and has no links to them.

Where are my games and saves?

In the Files app, under On My iPhone, then Tinbox: ROMs, Saves, States, Covers and more. Nothing is hidden and nothing is uploaded.

How do I back everything up?

Settings › Library › Back up saves and states. You can also copy the whole Tinbox folder out of the Files app any time.

Can I delete the included games?

Yes. Long-press a game in the Library and delete it like any other. They do not come back on their own.

How do save states, rewind and the Time Capsule work?

While playing, press MENU to open the Quick Menu. Save and load states, rewind the last seconds, toggle fast-forward, or open the Time Capsule to scrub back through automatic snapshots of your session.

Does the speedrun timer count against a personal best?

Yes. Turn it on from the Quick Menu. It keeps your best time for each game on your phone and shows how far ahead or behind you are.

What does signing in to RetroAchievements send?

Only what unlocking needs: a fingerprint of each game you open, the achievements you earn, and a short "now playing" status while a game runs. Nothing is sent while you are signed out. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do Bluetooth controllers work?

Yes. Pair any MFi or standard Bluetooth controller with your iPhone and it works in Tinbox, with remapping under Settings › Controls.

Does Tinbox collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The only network use is the optional box art download and, if you choose to sign in, RetroAchievements.
