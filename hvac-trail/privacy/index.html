The HVAC Trail: Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The HVAC Trail is a game. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information. This page explains exactly what the app keeps and where.

What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things on your device so the game can remember them between launches:

  • Your run in progress: saved as you play, so you can pick up where you left off after closing the app.
  • Your Hall of Fame: the top ten finished runs on this device, each with the lead tech name you typed, your role and your score. You can clear it from the Hall of Fame screen.
  • Your last setup: the crew names you typed, your van paint and whether sound is on, so the next run starts the way you like it.
  • Your garage: which vans you have unlocked.
  • Your best run, if you use Game Center: the lead tech name and company name you typed, your role, your score, how many of your crew made it and when you finished. The app keeps this on your phone to label your own row on the leaderboard.

None of this leaves your phone. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best final score is posted to the game's Game Center leaderboard, along with a number for the role you played. Other players see it next to your Game Center nickname. Game Center is run by Apple, and Apple's privacy policy covers it. The names you type for your crew and your company are never sent anywhere.

If you are not signed in, or you sign out in Settings, scores stay on your phone only.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts and no sign in of its own.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No third party code that collects data. The only outside frameworks are Apple's own.
  • No network requests of its own. The game and its fonts are built into the app and play offline.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
