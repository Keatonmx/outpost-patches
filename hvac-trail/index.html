The HVAC Trail

One American summer. Five techs. 530 miles of service calls between you and Labor Day.

A retro survival game about running residential air conditioning calls through the hottest months of the year, for iPhone. Written by a working HVAC tech, tech to tech.

Coming soon to the App Store.

Contact

Email kmaggard565@gmail.com. If you can, include the build number from TestFlight or the App Store version, and what screen you were on.

Questions

Does it need an account or the internet?

No. The whole game is inside the app and plays offline. There is no account to make.

What is the shop board?

If you are signed in to Game Center, your best final score posts to the game's Game Center leaderboard, under your Game Center nickname. If you aren't signed in, the Hall of Fame shows the scores saved on your phone.

Why did my run disappear when I closed the app?

The game doesn't save a run in progress yet. Finished runs, the Hall of Fame and your garage are kept.

How do I clear my scores and garage?

Delete the app and install it again. Nothing is stored anywhere else.

I found a bug or a call that isn't right.

Email the address above. Calls that a real tech would diagnose differently are especially welcome.
