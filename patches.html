How it works

  1. Choose your ROM file. A zipped ROM works too.
  2. Choose the patch file: .ips, .bps, .ups, .xdelta and the rest, or a .zip with the patch inside.
  3. Press Apply patch and save the patched game. If the patch says which ROM it needs, the patcher shows it, so you can check yours matches.
